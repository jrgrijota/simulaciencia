## What you will learn

With the changes of state simulation you will see, at the same time, what happens in a container and what its particles do when a substance is heated or cooled. You will find out what happens to the temperature during a change of state and why.

**How to work.** Each activity has three steps:

1. **Predict.** Before looking at the simulation, write down what you think will happen and why.
2. **Observe.** Set up the simulation, watch what happens and note down the data.
3. **Explain.** Compare what happened with your prediction and explain why.

If your prediction turns out to be wrong, don't rub it out: getting it wrong and understanding why is the best way to learn.

Each activity has a link that opens the simulation already set up. If you have the worksheet on paper, go to **simulaciencia.es/en/simulations/cambios-estado** and set it up as described under “Set up the simulation”.

**Controls.** Above the simulation there are “Restart” (back to the beginning), the hotplate (**❄ Cool**, **Off** or **🔥 Heat**) and the speed (×1, ×2, ×4). “Off” freezes the temperature so you can look calmly. The “Time” card gives the minutes since the start.
