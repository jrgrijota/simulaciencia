## What you will learn

With the circuits simulation you will build circuits with a battery, a switch and bulbs, and take measurements with an ammeter and a voltmeter. You will discover Ohm's law, which links voltage, current and resistance, and you will see what changes when the bulbs are connected one after another (in series) or each in its own branch (in parallel).

**How to work.** Each activity has three steps:

1. **Predict.** Before looking at the simulation, write down what you think will happen and why.
2. **Observe.** Set up the simulation, watch what happens and note down the data.
3. **Explain.** Compare what happened with your prediction and explain why.

If your prediction turns out to be wrong, don't rub it out: getting it wrong and understanding why is the best way to learn.

Each activity has a link that opens the simulation already set up. If you have the worksheet on paper, go to **simulaciencia.es/en/simulations/circuitos** and set it up as described under “Set up the simulation”.

**How to read the data.** Voltage (V) is measured in volts (V), current (I) in amperes (A), resistance (R) in ohms (Ω) and power (P) in watts (W). The “Components” table in the “Circuit readings” panel gives R, V, I and P for each bulb and for the total.
