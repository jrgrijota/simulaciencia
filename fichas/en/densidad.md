## What you will learn

With the density simulation you will find out why two objects of the same size can have different masses, what density is and how it tells you whether something floats in water.

**How to work.** Each activity has three steps:

1. **Predict.** Before looking at the simulation, write down what you think will happen and why.
2. **Observe.** Set up the simulation, watch what happens and note down the data.
3. **Explain.** Compare what happened with your prediction and explain why.

If your prediction turns out to be wrong, don't rub it out: getting it wrong and understanding why is the best way to learn.

Each activity has a link that opens the simulation already set up. If you have the worksheet on paper, go to **simulaciencia.es/en/simulations/densidad** and set it up as described under “Set up the simulation”.

**Units.** Volume is in litres (L), mass in kilograms (kg) and density in kilograms per litre (kg/L). One litre of water has a mass of 1 kg.
