## What you will learn

With the mechanical energy simulation you will ride a roller coaster and let go of a pendulum. You will see how the energy of height (potential) turns into the energy of movement (kinetic) and back again, you will check that their sum is conserved and you will find out where the energy goes when there is friction.

**How to work.** Each activity has three steps:

1. **Predict.** Before looking at the simulation, write down what you think will happen and why.
2. **Observe.** Set up the simulation, watch what happens and note down the data.
3. **Explain.** Compare what happened with your prediction and explain why.

If your prediction turns out to be wrong, don't rub it out: getting it wrong and understanding why is the best way to learn.

Each activity has a link that opens the simulation already set up. If you have the worksheet on paper, go to **simulaciencia.es/en/simulations/energia-mecanica** and set it up as described under “Set up the simulation”.

**How to read the data.** The “Real-time readings” panel gives the speed (v), the height (h) and the energies: kinetic (Ek, green), potential (Ep, blue) and mechanical (Em = Ek + Ep, yellow). With friction, heat appears (Q, orange). To read a value at a particular point, set the “Playback speed” to ×¼ and press “⏸ Pause” at that point.
