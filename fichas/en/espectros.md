## What you will learn

With the spectra simulation you will see how an atom absorbs and emits light. You will find out that the electron can only be in certain energy levels and that this is why each element emits particular colours, which are like its fingerprint.

**How to work.** Each activity has three steps:

1. **Predict.** Before looking at the simulation, write down what you think will happen and why.
2. **Observe.** Set up the simulation, watch what happens and note down the data.
3. **Explain.** Compare what happened with your prediction and explain why.

If your prediction turns out to be wrong, don't rub it out: getting it wrong and understanding why is the best way to learn.

Each activity has a link that opens the simulation already set up. If you have the worksheet on paper, go to **simulaciencia.es/en/simulations/espectros** and set it up as described under “Set up the simulation”.

**Data.** The wavelength (λ) of light is measured in nanometres (nm). Visible light goes from about 380 nm (violet) to about 780 nm (red). Energy is measured in electronvolts (eV). The energy of a photon is **E = h · c / λ**, with h = 6.63 · 10⁻³⁴ J·s, c = 3.00 · 10⁸ m/s and 1 eV = 1.60 · 10⁻¹⁹ J.
