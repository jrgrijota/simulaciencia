## What you will learn

With the gases simulation you will see a gas for what it is: lots of particles that never stop moving and keep hitting the walls. You will find out what pressure is and how the pressure and volume of a gas change when you heat it, compress it or add more gas.

**How to work.** Each activity has three steps:

1. **Predict.** Before looking at the simulation, write down what you think will happen and why.
2. **Observe.** Set up the simulation, watch what happens and note down the data.
3. **Explain.** Compare what happened with your prediction and explain why.

If your prediction turns out to be wrong, don't rub it out: getting it wrong and understanding why is the best way to learn.

Each activity has a link that opens the simulation already set up. If you have the worksheet on paper, go to **simulaciencia.es/en/simulations/gases** and set it up as described under “Set up the simulation”.

**How to read the data.** Pressure (P) is in atmospheres (atm), volume (V) in litres (L) and temperature (T) in kelvin (K). The pressure goes up and down a little every second because it is calculated from the collisions in that second: **wait a few seconds after each change and write down a middle value**.
